My view

The arcade cabinet and the auction house are the two that feel like nobody else's. The arcade suits the people most likely to play and makes the pixel crown look native. The auction house is the bolder, calmer choice and would stand out most in a feed. The departure board is the best idea for the price itself, and its flipping digits could be borrowed into either of the other two.

Tell me which one or two you want taken further, or what to mix. I'd then draw the chosen one properly, with the phone layout and the other two pages, before anything on the live site changes.
