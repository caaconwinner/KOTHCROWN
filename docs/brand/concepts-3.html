The w is the crown, and it's slipping

Crown stays, pixels go. This round is only about the idea you liked: the w in "crown" drawn as the crown, and tilted. Six ways to draw that one letter, then my pick at work.

Why tilt it

A crown that sits straight is a crown someone is wearing comfortably. A crooked one is about to come off. That is the product: whoever holds it is always about to lose it. So the tilt is not decoration, and it can move: straight the moment the crown is taken, slipping further as the price melts.

Six ways to draw the w

Each is shown in the word, then alone at three sizes. Same tilt on all of them so only the drawing differs.

My pick: the real w

It is the only one that is honestly a letter. The others are crowns placed where a w should be; this is a w that turns out to be a crown once it tips over and gets its three dots. Number 2 is the safer choice if you want it to say "crown" with no word next to it.
