A crown that melts

Six logo directions for KOTH Crown, one recommendation, and the colours, motion and media that follow from it. Nothing here is final: tell me what you like and what to drop.

What the product means

This isn't just a fee-sharing token. It turns a revenue stream into something you hold while it gets cheaper for everyone else, so the question is never "is it worth it" but "how long can I keep it".

Every crypto project with a ruler metaphor already has a crown. What only this one has is the melt: the price falls every second, stops at a floor set by real fee income, and snaps back up when someone takes it. The identity should be about that clock, with the crown as the thing the clock eats.

MeltingIce, wax, pixels falling off. The price as a physical thing that shrinks. Most ownable, and it is the mechanic itself.
The bracketThe site already speaks in [brackets]. A throne is a seat between two walls. Cheap to produce, native to the page.
The letterThe word "crown" already contains a crown: the w has three points. No extra icon needed.
The hillKing of the hill is a playground game. A summit, one person on it, everyone else climbing.
The countdownA ring that drains. Honest about the timer, but every app has a progress ring.
Passed overCoins, candles, rockets, thrones with jewels, lions. Generic to crypto or to royalty, and none of them says "melts".

Recommendation: pixel melt, with the crown as the w

Directions 1 and 2 are the same idea at two sizes, so I would use both. The icon is the pixel crown. In the wordmark, that same crown stands in for the w. One shape to remember, and it is the only shape on the site that moves.

kothcron

At 24px and below the drips are dropped and the bare crown is used. The wordmark above is set in Inter with the icon dropped in; a final version would have the w spaced and sized by hand.

  • Why this one. It could not be moved onto another project: the missing pixels are the price falling and the band is the floor. It also fits the plain, low-fi page in the design notes without borrowing anyone's assets.
  • What it gives up. It is not elegant, and a pixel crown on its own is a familiar emoji-like shape. The drips, the band, and above all the behaviour are what make it ours, so the static mark needs the motion more than the others do.
  • Runner-up. Direction 3, the bracket throne, if you want something quieter that leans on the site's typography instead.

How the mark behaves

The crown on the throne page is the melt bar. It is drawn from the same on-chain checkpoint as the price, so it needs no extra data.

crown price · illustration, not live data 3.41 SOL
melting 4%/min
after a takeoverFull crown, gold. Card shakes for 0.4s and flashes gold for 0.6s.
meltingPixels drain from the body and the points sink, in step with price between the takeover price and the floor. It stays a crown until the last row.
melt at 6%/min or moreCrown turns ember, then red: volume is dropping and it is getting cheap fast.
at the floorOnly the band is left. It never disappears, because the price never goes below the floor.
reduced motionNo shake and no sliding: pixels fade, the flash stays.

Colour

The palette in the design notes holds up, so this keeps it and gives each colour one job. The only addition is ember, a step between gold and red for the melt states. Flat fills only, no gradients.

ground#1b1d28Page. Always.
card#2e303aCards, skeletons.
gold#facc15The crown and the takeover flash. Nothing else.
ember#fb923cNew. Crown and bar at 6 to 8%/min.
red#f87171Crown and bar at 8%/min, near the floor.
mint#86efacMoney: prices, fees, the primary button.
sky#93c5fdPeople: wallet addresses.
muted#9ca3afLabels and secondary text.

Rule of thumb: gold means the crown, mint means SOL, sky means a person. If gold shows up on a button or a link, it stops meaning the crown. Type stays Inter, with tabular figures for anything that ticks; a monospace (JetBrains Mono here) is optional for bracketed actions and addresses.

Voice

Lowercase, short, no exclamation marks, numbers always with units. It should sound like the page looks.

Things to check before committing

  • The name. "King of the hill" was the label pump.fun used for its top coin in 2024. It is a generic phrase and a playground game, but "KOTH" next to a pump.fun coin will read as a reference to them. That may be exactly what you want; it is worth deciding on purpose, since the project rule is not to use another platform's name.
  • Nothing is cleared. I have not checked trademarks, domains or handles for "KOTH Crown" or for the mark. That needs doing before anything is printed or registered.
  • Legal copy. Lines like "earn the fees" describe a revenue right. The spec already flags getting advice on that before launch copy goes out.
  • Still a sketch. These are drawn in code to compare ideas. The chosen mark should be redrawn carefully (pixel grid, drip lengths, wordmark spacing) and exported as SVG and PNG at the sizes above.
