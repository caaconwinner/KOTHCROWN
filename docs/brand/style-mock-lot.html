  • The price is the headline. It sits under the title at about three times the size it has today, with the melt bar directly beneath it.
  • One sentence says what you are buying, right under the name, so a first-time visitor doesn't need the how-it-works page to get it.
  • "King" becomes "held by". The words follow the auction: lot, holder, provenance, sale. Say if you'd rather keep king, reign and takeovers and only take the layout.
  • Fewer rows. Held by, held for, earned this reign, fees now. The floor moves into the line under the price. All-time fees and total burned are left off here; they could go on the past-holders page or come back as two more rows.
  • Each provenance entry keeps everything the feed shows today: who, from whom, price, how long the last holder kept it, what they earned, and what was burned.
  • Unchanged: the crown still sinks into its band as the price falls, gold is still only the crown, mint is money, sky is people.
