# Design: early-memecoin "King of the Hill" look

The site should feel like the 2024-era memecoin launchpad homepages, where a "king of the hill" coin sat at the top of a dark, low-fi page. Inspired by that era. **Do not copy any platform's logo, wordmark, name or exact assets.** Our own name, our own crown mark.

## What the original looked like (research notes)

Measured from an archived June 2024 snapshot of that homepage:
- Page background: `#1b1d28` (rgb 27, 29, 40), measured.
- One job per colour: gold means the crown, mint means SOL, sky means a person. Gold on a button or a link is a mistake. Flat fills only.

Font: Inter, measured.
- Text links written in square brackets: `[twitter] [support] [telegram]` top-left, and a big centered `[start a new coin]`.
- Search field filled with pale mint green (about `#a6e9b0`), dark text, with a separate green `search` button.
- Loading skeleton blocks in slate gray (about `#2e303a`).
- The "king of the hill" coin sat centered at the top: coin image on the left, details on the right (name, ticker, created by, market cap, replies).

From memory of the era (not measured, confirm with screenshots if possible):
- Market cap text in green, "created by" usernames in a light blue.
- New activity made a coin card shake and flash yellow (the "bump" animation).
- Very plain, almost unstyled: no gradients, no shadows, small rounded corners, lots of dark space.

## Name and logo

- Project name: **take the crown**. Ticker: **$CROWN**. Wordmark: **crown**. No "KOTH" anywhere on the site.
- The mark is a pixel crown, 11 by 6 pixels, with no drips. The bottom row is the band: it stands for the floor and never melts.
- In the wordmark the same crown stands in for the w. It stands on the baseline and is 15% taller than the lowercase letters.
- Files are generated by `node scripts/brand/build.cjs` into `docs/brand/assets/` (SVG and PNG: icon, wordmark, favicon, profile picture, coin image, link preview, X header). The site's copies are in `app/public/brand/`. Do not edit the outputs by hand; change the script.
- How it was chosen: `docs/brand/concepts.html` (round 1). Rounds 2 and 3 were not taken.

## Layout: the throne page `/`

Laid out like an auction lot, in the site's own dark style. Two columns on desktop, one on a phone (crown first).

```
[how it works] [hall of kings]                          [connect wallet]

                              crown (wordmark)

gold · under 6% a minute       ┌───────────────┐   the crown
                                │               │   pays the king 85% of the fees on every $CROWN trade.
orange · 6 to 8% a minute      │  [crown img]  │   sold to whoever pays the price below. the last king
                                │               │   receives 90% of it.
red · 8% a minute              └───────────────┘
  all-time fees  212.40 SOL
  burned          18.20 SOL   3.4100 SOL ↓            (the headline)
                              ██████████░░░░
                              falling 4% a minute · will not go below 1.4000 SOL
                              king                 7xKp…Q2a
                              reigning             4m 12s
                              earned this reign    1.8200 SOL
                              fees now             0.1400 SOL a minute
                              [        take the crown        ]
                              you pay at most 3.4441 SOL · limit +0.5% +1% +3%

  takeovers ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [card] 7xKp… took it from FiR2… for 4.20 SOL · old king reigned 9m and
         earned 1.10 SOL · 0.42 SOL burned · 2m ago
```

- The price is the headline. The floor lives in the line under the melt bar.
- Words: king, reigning, earned this reign, takeovers, hall of kings. No auction wording ("lot 1 · always for sale" was removed at the owner's request).
- Colour legend: the three melt colours, and what each means, as text only (no arrows). The one the crown shows now is lit, the other two are faded. On wide screens (1280px and up) it sits in the empty space left of the page; narrower, it goes under the crown.
- All-time fees and burned sit under the crown.

## Motion

- **Bump:** when a `CrownTaken` event arrives, the lot shakes (CSS `transform: translateX` keyframes, ~400ms) and the crown's plate flashes `--gold` for ~600ms. The new feed card slides in at the top with the same flash.
- **Ticking price:** updates every second from the local melt calculation.
- **The crown is the melt gauge.** The crown on the king card keeps the logo's shape but is drawn from small pixels (each logo pixel is 3 by 3 of them, 315 above the band). As the price falls from the last takeover price toward the floor they leave one at a time, each leaving a hole where it was, scattered all over the crown with a slight lean toward the top. A pixel that leaves falls from its place in the crown all the way to the bottom of the page, over the rest of the content, accelerating like something dropped; it never blocks a click and is removed when it lands. Nothing moves between losses: there is no constant dripping. At the floor only the band is left, and the band never loses a pixel. A takeover puts every pixel back. Pixels already missing when the page opens are simply absent, so loading the page does not start with a shower.
- **Melt bar:** shrinks toward the floor. Crown and bar share a colour: gold below 6%/min, `--ember` from 6%/min, `--red` at 8%/min.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: no shake and no falling pixels: a lost pixel just disappears, and the color flash stays.

## Copy style

Lowercase, short, bracketed actions: `[take the crown]`, `[how it works]`, `[hall of kings]`. Numbers always with units ("3.41 SOL").
